SMS Missed-Call Alerts

The Shirtless Handyman LLC sends automated text alerts to the business owner when a call to the business goes unanswered, with the caller's number and any matching lead details, so every call gets returned fast. This program is for the business owner only. Customers and callers do not receive texts.

To sign up, text ALERTS to (504) 230-0550.

By texting ALERTS to (504) 230-0550, you agree to receive recurring automated missed-call alert text messages from The Shirtless Handyman LLC. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

After you text ALERTS, you'll receive: "The Shirtless Handyman: You're signed up for missed-call alerts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
