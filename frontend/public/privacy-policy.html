Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Shirtless Handyman LLC ("The Shirtless Handyman," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit theshirtlesshandyman.com, contact us, or receive text messages from us. The Shirtless Handyman is a microcement and Venetian plaster company serving the New Orleans metro and the Northshore.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, and project address or ZIP code when you fill out a form, call, text, or email us.
  • Project details: information and photos you share about your project.
  • Call and message information: the phone number, date, and time of calls and texts to our business number.
  • Basic website data: standard technical information such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry, schedule site visits, and prepare quotes.
  • To return missed calls and follow up on your project.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive, such as missed-call alerts and service notifications.
  • To operate and improve our website and services.

SMS / text messaging

The Shirtless Handyman sends text messages only to people who have opted in. Our missed-call alert program sends automated alerts to the business owner when a call goes unanswered. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. To sign up for missed-call alerts, text ALERTS to (504) 230-0550 (see SMS alerts sign-up).

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, and opt-in data are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share information only with service providers that help us deliver messages and run our business (for example, our phone and text messaging provider), and only as needed to provide those services.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with service providers who work on our behalf (such as messaging, email, and website hosting providers), when required by law, or to protect our rights and safety.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing us.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
